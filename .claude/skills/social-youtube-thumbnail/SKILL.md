# YouTube Thumbnail — Cover Tester

Designs and scores thumbnail concepts so you test the strongest three.

Department: **Social Media**

## When to use
Use when preparing a video thumbnail or choosing between cover options.

## How to work
1. Write three concepts with a different emotion or angle each.
2. Keep text to 4 words or fewer, large and high-contrast.
3. Check it reads at phone size (about 160px wide).
4. Score each concept 1-5 on clarity, emotion, contrast and fit with the title.
5. Recommend the top two to A/B test.

## Output
Three concepts with scores and a test plan.

## Guardrails
Thumbnails must match the video content; no misleading imagery.
