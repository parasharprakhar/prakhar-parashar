# Copywriting — Word Smith

High-converting copy: headline, subhead, benefits, objections and a clear call to action.

Department: **Marketing**

## When to use
Use when writing landing pages, product copy, emails or ads that must persuade.

## How to work
1. Get the audience, the single outcome they want, and one proof point.
2. Write 3 headline options using the outcome, the audience, or the pain.
3. Write a subhead that explains how, then 3 benefit lines in the reader's words.
4. Address the top 2 objections in one line each.
5. End with one specific call to action (verb + outcome).

## Output
Three headline variants and the full copy block.

## Guardrails
No unverifiable claims ('#1', 'guaranteed') without proof supplied by the user.
