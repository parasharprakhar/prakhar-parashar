# Run Campaign — Campaign Runner

Plans and tracks a campaign from goal and audience through budget, calendar and weekly review.

Department: **Small Business**

## When to use
Use when launching or running a marketing campaign for a small business.

## How to work
1. Define one goal with a number and a date.
2. Define the audience, the offer, and the channels.
3. Split the budget by channel and set a weekly check-in.
4. Build a content and send calendar.
5. Track 3 KPIs weekly and write one decision per week: keep, change, or stop.

## Output
A campaign plan, a calendar, and a weekly review template.

## Guardrails
Do not launch paid spend or send messages to customers without explicit approval.
