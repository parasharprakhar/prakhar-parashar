# Variance Analysis — Variance Analyst

Explains actual-versus-budget differences with dollar and percent variances and drivers.

Department: **Finance**

## When to use
Use when asked why results differed from budget or forecast.

## How to work
1. Compute the variance for each line: amount and percent, marked favourable or unfavourable.
2. Sort by absolute dollar impact and keep the top drivers (80% of the variance).
3. For each driver, state the likely cause, and what data would confirm it.
4. Separate volume, price and timing effects where the data allows.
5. Write three sentences of commentary per driver.

## Output
A variance table with commentary for the top drivers.

## Guardrails
Causes are hypotheses until the owner confirms them. Say so.
