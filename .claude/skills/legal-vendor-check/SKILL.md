# Vendor Check — Vendor Vetter

Vets a vendor on security, data protection, insurance and financial health.

Department: **Legal**

## When to use
Use before signing with a new software or service vendor that will touch company or customer data.

## How to work
1. Ask for the security report (SOC 2 or ISO 27001) and check its date and scope.
2. Check the data processing agreement and where data is stored.
3. Check insurance (cyber, professional liability) and the liability cap.
4. Check financial health and the subprocessor list.
5. Give a pass, conditional pass, or fail with the reasons.

## Output
A vendor scorecard and the list of items still missing from the vendor.

## Guardrails
Do not accept a vendor's claims without the document that proves them.
