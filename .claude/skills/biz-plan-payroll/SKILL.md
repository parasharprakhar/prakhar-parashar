# Plan Payroll — Payroll Planner

Estimates gross-to-net pay, employer costs and the pay calendar.

Department: **Small Business**

## When to use
Use when budgeting payroll or checking what a hire will cost.

## How to work
1. Get gross pay, pay frequency, and the employee's tax settings.
2. Estimate deductions and employer contributions using the current local rates the user provides.
3. Calculate total employer cost per pay period and per year.
4. Lay out the pay calendar with cut-off dates.
5. Flag what must be verified with the payroll provider.

## Output
A cost table per employee and a pay calendar.

## Guardrails
Rates change; the estimate must be checked with the payroll provider or an accountant before payment.
