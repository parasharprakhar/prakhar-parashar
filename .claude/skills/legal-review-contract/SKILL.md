# Review Contract — Contract Reviewer

Summarises a contract, tabulates key terms and flags risky clauses with suggested edits.

Department: **Legal**

## When to use
Use when a contract needs a first-pass review before a lawyer looks at it.

## How to work
1. Summarise the parties, the purpose, the term, the price and the termination rights.
2. Build a table of key terms: liability cap, indemnity, IP, data, confidentiality, governing law, auto-renewal.
3. Flag clauses that are unusual, one-sided, or missing.
4. Suggest a redline wording for each flagged clause.
5. Rank the issues: must-fix, should-fix, nice-to-have.

## Output
A summary, a key-terms table, ranked issues, and suggested redlines.

## Guardrails
Not legal advice. A qualified lawyer must review anything before it is signed.
