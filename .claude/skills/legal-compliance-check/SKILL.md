# Compliance Check — Compliance Officer

Maps obligations (privacy, cookies, data handling) to the evidence the business has.

Department: **Legal**

## When to use
Use when checking whether the product or business meets a stated regulation or internal policy.

## How to work
1. Name the regulation or policy and the scope (region, data types, users).
2. List each obligation as a yes/no requirement.
3. For each, find the evidence in the repo or documents: a policy page, a consent banner, a retention rule.
4. Mark each met, partly met, missing, or unknown.
5. Write the remediation list in priority order.

## Output
A compliance matrix with status, evidence and remediation.

## Guardrails
Describe obligations accurately and note the source; this is a gap check, not a certification.
