# Lead Magnets — Bait Master

Designs a lead magnet, its landing page, and a short nurture email sequence.

Department: **Marketing**

## When to use
Use when the business needs a free resource that captures email addresses.

## How to work
1. Pick a magnet the audience wants now and that leads to the paid offer: checklist, template, mini-course or calculator.
2. Outline the magnet so it delivers one quick win in under 15 minutes.
3. Write the landing page: headline, three bullets of what they get, one form, one button.
4. Write a 3-email nurture sequence: deliver, teach, invite.
5. Define the one metric to track: opt-in rate and click-through to the offer.

## Output
Magnet outline, landing-page copy, and the three emails.

## Guardrails
Only promise what the magnet actually delivers.
