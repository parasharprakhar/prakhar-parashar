# Margin Analyzer — Margin Analyst

Calculates gross and contribution margin by product and shows what a price change does.

Department: **Small Business**

## When to use
Use when asking which products make money or what a price change would do.

## How to work
1. Get price, unit cost, variable costs and volume per product.
2. Compute gross margin % = (price - COGS) / price.
3. Compute contribution margin after variable selling costs.
4. Model a price change of plus or minus 5% and 10%, with the volume loss that would break even.
5. Rank products by contribution dollars, not margin percent alone.

## Output
A margin table per product and a price sensitivity table.
