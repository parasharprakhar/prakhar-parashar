# Customer Research — Voice of Customer

Turns interviews, reviews and tickets into themes, quotes and the customer's own language.

Department: **Marketing**

## When to use
Use when there is raw customer feedback and the team needs patterns and quotes.

## How to work
1. Gather the raw text and note the source of each item.
2. Tag each item with a pain, a desired outcome, a trigger, or an objection.
3. Group tags into 3-7 themes; count how many sources support each theme.
4. Pull the exact phrases customers use, for copy and headlines.
5. Write the jobs-to-be-done statements the themes point to.

## Output
A theme report with counts, verbatim quotes and jobs-to-be-done.

## Guardrails
Quote accurately. Never invent a customer quote or a number.
