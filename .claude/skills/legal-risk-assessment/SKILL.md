# Legal Risk Assessment — Risk Assessor

Scores legal risks by likelihood and impact and lists mitigations.

Department: **Legal**

## When to use
Use when a new product, deal, or market entry needs a risk view.

## How to work
1. List the risks in plain language, one per row.
2. Score likelihood and impact 1-5 each; multiply for the score.
3. Sort by score and mark anything scoring 15 or above as high.
4. Write one mitigation per high risk with an owner.
5. Note which risks need a lawyer's opinion.

## Output
A risk register with scores, mitigations and owners.

## Guardrails
Scores are judgement calls; say what information would change them.
