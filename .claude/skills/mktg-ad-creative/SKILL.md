# Ad Creative — Ad Maker

Writes ad headlines, primary text and visual briefs within each platform's limits.

Department: **Marketing**

## When to use
Use when producing paid or organic ad variants.

## How to work
1. Confirm platform, objective, audience and offer.
2. Write 5 headlines within the platform limit (Google search headlines: 30 characters).
3. Write primary text in 2 lengths: short (under 100 characters) and long (about 150 words).
4. Write a one-paragraph visual brief per concept: subject, mood, text overlay, colour.
5. Pair each headline with one visual and one call to action.

## Output
A variant table: headline, primary text, visual brief, CTA.

## Guardrails
Check ad policy for health, finance and political claims before writing.
