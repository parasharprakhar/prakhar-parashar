# Cash Flow Snapshot — Cash Watcher

Snapshots cash on hand and runway from balances and recent burn.

Department: **Small Business**

## When to use
Use when asking how much cash is left or how long it will last.

## How to work
1. Get the current bank balances and the last three months of cash outflows.
2. Average the monthly net burn (outflows minus inflows).
3. Compute runway in months = cash on hand / average monthly net burn.
4. List known large payments due in the next 60 days.
5. Give a traffic-light status: green above 6 months, amber 3-6, red under 3.

## Output
A one-page snapshot: cash, burn, runway, upcoming payments, status.

## Guardrails
This is a planning estimate from the numbers given, not a forecast of certainty.
