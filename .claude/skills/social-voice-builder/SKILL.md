# Voice Builder — Voice Coach

Extracts a writing voice from samples into a usable style guide.

Department: **Social Media**

## When to use
Use when someone wants content that sounds like them, or a team needs one consistent voice.

## How to work
1. Collect 3-5 samples of the person's writing.
2. Measure average sentence length, paragraph length, and how often they use questions and lists.
3. Note recurring words, humour style, and phrases they never use.
4. Write a one-page voice guide with 5 rules and 5 example sentences.
5. Test the guide by drafting one short paragraph and checking it sounds right.

## Output
A voice guide with rules, examples, and banned phrases.

## Guardrails
Use the samples the user provided only. Do not copy distinctive text from other writers.
