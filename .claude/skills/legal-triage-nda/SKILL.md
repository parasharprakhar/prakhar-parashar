# Triage NDA — NDA Triage

Fast traffic-light review of a non-disclosure agreement against standard checks.

Department: **Legal**

## When to use
Use when an NDA arrives and the team needs to decide whether it can be signed quickly.

## How to work
1. Check parties and whether it is mutual or one-way.
2. Check the definition of confidential information and the standard exclusions.
3. Check the term, the survival period, and the return-or-destroy duty.
4. Check governing law, venue, and any non-solicit or residuals clause.
5. Mark each check green, yellow or red, and give an overall verdict.

## Output
A one-page checklist with a traffic light per check and an overall verdict.

## Guardrails
Red items go to counsel. Do not sign or accept on the user's behalf.
